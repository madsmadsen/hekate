# Code of conduct

This code of conduct is a short form of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to take part in this project without harassment. This applies to people of every age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, social and economic class, nationality, appearance, race, religion, and sexual identity and orientation.

We act and interact in ways that help an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Good behavior:

- Be kind and patient with other people.
- Respect different opinions, points of view, and experiences.
- Give feedback that helps, and accept feedback with grace.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes, say sorry to the people you hurt, and learn from the mistake.
- Think about what is best for the whole community, not only for you.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Sexual words or pictures, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing the private information of other people, such as a postal address or an email address, without their permission.
- Other conduct that people would reasonably call unprofessional.

## Our duties

The maintainers explain what good behavior is. They act in a fair way when behavior is not acceptable. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct. They explain their reasons.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all spaces of the project, and when a person speaks for the project in public.

## Report a problem

Tell the repository maintainers about unacceptable behavior. Use a private GitHub security advisory, or use the email address in the profile of a maintainer. The maintainers review every report quickly and fairly. They keep the person who reports private.

## What happens next

The maintainers choose one or more of these steps:

1. **Correction.** A private message that explains the problem. They may ask for an apology.
2. **Warning.** A warning that says what the consequences of more bad behavior are.
3. **Temporary ban.** No contact with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban.** No part in the project from now on.
